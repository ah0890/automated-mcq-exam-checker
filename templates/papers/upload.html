Scanning tips

  • Keep all four corner squares visible
    They are used to straighten each page. One cropped corner is tolerated.
  • Scan at 200–300 dpi
    Grayscale is fine. Phone photos work if the sheet is flat and evenly lit.
  • Any order, any orientation
    Sideways and upside-down pages are corrected automatically.
  • Re-uploading is safe
    Files and pages already in the examination are detected and skipped.
